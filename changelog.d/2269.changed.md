- **feat(exceptions): excepted results reach the auditor and the board, and follow the exception at once (#2269).** The OSCAL assessment results now carry each excepted cell as a risk-accepted observation linked to the exception that accepted it (id, expiry, approver, in back-matter), never as a finding. The board pack's coverage-trend sentence states how much of the quarter-end coverage and of the generated baseline rests only on exceptions. Activating, revoking or expiring an exception re-evaluates its control as soon as the change commits, recorded under the new evaluation trigger `exception_change` (migration `20261240000000`). "Covered by exception" counts only scope cells in a control's latest evaluation run, so a cell that stops applying stops counting, and the dashboard tile's tooltip says one excepted cell is enough for a control to count.
