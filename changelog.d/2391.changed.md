- **fix(risks): creating a risk with a proposed acceptance needs a justification and a proposed expiry (#2391).** `POST /v1/risks` and the MCP `create_risk` tool now take `justification` and `proposed_until` (YYYY-MM-DD) with `acceptance_status=proposed`, and require both, as `PATCH /v1/risks/{id}/acceptance/propose` does: a missing justification, or a proposed expiry that is missing or not after today, is a 400 and nothing is written. Both are stored on the acceptance, as a propose records them. Sending either without `acceptance_status=proposed` is also a 400. MCP refuses such a proposal when it is filed. The risk create form asks for both when the treatment is Accept. Upgrade note: clients that created proposed acceptances without these fields must now send them.
