- **feat(controls): control owners are readable and filterable (#2128).** The person "assign owner" sets is now returned as `owner_user_id` on `GET /v1/controls?include=state` and on control detail (`GET /v1/controls/{id}/coverage`), and `?owner=<user id>` filters the controls list to that person's controls. The controls list gains an Owner column and the control page shows the Owner by name; the manifest `owner_role` is shown as the default owner role.
