- **fix(actionplans): tombstoning an action plan, or unlinking a risk from one, no longer leaves a mitigate risk with no mitigation (#2449).** A mitigate risk keeps at least one linked control or live action plan. The control side already enforced that (#2444); `DELETE /v1/action-plans/{id}` and `DELETE /v1/action-plans/{id}/risks/{risk_id}` now lock each affected risk row, re-check the rule after the change, and refuse with 409 naming the open risk that would be left with neither. A closed risk does not block either. A tombstone that touches several risks is refused as a whole if any one would break. Two concurrent writes that each remove one of a risk's two plans now serialize on the risk row: one succeeds and the other is refused. Existing data is not repaired.
