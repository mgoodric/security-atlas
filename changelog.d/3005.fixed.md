- **fix(eval, personnelsecurity, risk): evidence-ingest subscribers term deterministic failures instead of redelivering them forever, and a score PATCH recomputes the residual (#3005).** The eval subscriber now Acks a record for a control that does not exist (an unwrapped scope error NAKed it forever) and terms a control that cannot be evaluated as stored: a malformed `applicability_expr`, `evidence_queries` or exception predicate, or an evidence query that does not compile, fails the SQL guard or returns an invalid result. These are counted on `atlas.eval.control_unevaluable`. A worker event bound to a control UUID the tenant does not have now creates its personnel-security checklist without a control, as an SCF-anchor ref already does, where it used to redeliver forever. It is logged and counted on `atlas.personnelsecurity.unknown_control`. The residual subscriber reads a linked control's existing evaluations when the control cannot be re-evaluated, and terms the risk when the control cannot be read. Every NAK on the stream now backs off from 2s, doubling per delivery to 5 minutes. `PATCH /v1/risks/{id}` that changes `inherent_score` or `methodology` on a risk with linked controls recomputes its stored residual.
