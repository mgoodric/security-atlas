- **fix(api): a stuck gRPC stream no longer holds `atlas` shutdown forever (#2805).** On SIGTERM, or when a fatal subsystem cancels the run group (#2781), gRPC `Run` called `GracefulStop()` with no deadline, so one long-lived or stuck stream kept the process from exiting for its supervisor to restart. gRPC now drains for at most `api.ShutdownTimeout` (5 s, the same constant HTTP's drain now uses) and then calls `Stop()`. A supervisor's stop timeout should allow at least 5 s; Docker's default 10 s does.
