- **feat(risks): link and unlink controls, and add an action plan, from the risk page (#2106).** The risk page has a Linked controls section: link controls with the shared control picker and unlink one, confirmed inline. A new route, `DELETE /v1/risks/{id}/controls/{control_id}`, unlinks a control. Linking and unlinking are written to the risk's history (`control_linked`, `control_unlinked`; the unified log names the field only), and both are refused on a closed risk (409). Unlinking the last control of a mitigate risk with no live action plan is refused (400). "Add action plan" opens the new-plan form with the risk already linked and returns to the risk once the plan is created. Migration `20261220000000_risk_control_link_audit.sql` adds the two audit actions.
