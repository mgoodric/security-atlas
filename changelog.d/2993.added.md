- **feat(connectors/aws): S3 bucket policies that deny non-TLS requests; a new CC6.7 control checks them (#2993).** `aws-connector run --kind aws.s3.bucket_tls_enforcement.v1` calls `GetBucketPolicy` per bucket and pushes one `aws.s3.bucket_tls_enforcement.v1` record each, with `policy_present` and `denies_insecure_transport`. A bucket passes when its policy denies every principal and every S3 action on the bucket and its objects under `Bool aws:SecureTransport "false"`, the statement in the S3 user guide. No policy, or a policy without that statement, fails. An unreadable or unparsable policy is inconclusive. The kind is routed by type.
