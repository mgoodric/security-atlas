- **fix(eval): a replay of a past date applies the exceptions in force at that date (#3037).** Replay used today's active exceptions, so granting, revoking or expiring an exception after T changed the replay of T although nothing at T had changed. A replay now reads the control's exceptions activated by T and not revoked by T (`ListExceptionsByControlAsOf`), whatever their status is now, and judges their `[effective_from, expires_at)` window at T. This is the rule the OSCAL export already uses to name the exception behind an excepted cell. Live evaluation still reads the active set and judges it at the wall clock. The audit-period freeze does not read exceptions and is unchanged. No migration.
