- **fix(authz): the per-control evidence list's `total` is the filtered count, and an auditor's rows match its authz lookup (#2470).** `GET /v1/evidence?control_id=…` returned the tenant-wide evidence ledger count as `total`, so an auditor admitted for one control in one period learned how much evidence the whole tenant holds. `total` is now the number of records matching the request's control and `since`/`until` window, for every role. A period-scoped caller (an auditor) also resolves the control by `control_id` alone, the lookup its authz decision covered, so a record filed under another control whose `control_ref` names this one is no longer listed to it. Admins, grc_engineers, approvers and control_owners keep the existing `control_id` or `control_ref` resolution; only their `total` changes. The tenant-wide list (`GET /v1/evidence` without `control_id`) is unchanged.
