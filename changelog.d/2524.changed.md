- **feat(policies): any member acknowledges a policy aimed at them, and a policy's audience is a role or Everyone (#2524).** A viewer, auditor or control owner can now acknowledge a published policy that targets their live role, for themselves only; before, only admins and GRC engineers could. A policy's acknowledgment audience is now chosen from the five roles plus **Everyone (all members)**. A new policy that names any other value is refused (400). An audience written before this change (for example "employee") still shows on the policy, flagged "unrecognised audience", and a draft can keep it, but no one holds it as a role, so no one is asked to acknowledge it until it is changed. The stock Access Control and Information Security policies now target Everyone. Existing policies are not changed; `scripts/dry-run-2524-policy-audiences.sql` counts the free-text audiences in a deployment.
