- **feat(controls): stock controls cc1_5, cc5_3 and cc7_4 check acknowledgement coverage in SQL, and acknowledgements record the policy's title and version (#2832, #1860).** `POST /v1/policies/{id}/acknowledge` now adds the acknowledged policy's `policy_title` and `policy_version` to its `policy.acknowledgment.v1` record (both already optional in schema 1.0.0). `soc2_cc1_5_accountability_acknowledgment` fails when any user who has acknowledged something has not acknowledged all five stock policies by title, and passes when every such user has. `soc2_cc5_3_policies_procedures` fails when an acknowledgement names a version its policy had already moved past. `soc2_cc7_4_incident_response` passes when someone has acknowledged the Incident Response Plan and is otherwise inconclusive. Before, each passed on any acknowledgement of any policy. Titles are matched exactly, so a renamed stock policy no longer counts. Users with no acknowledgement at all, and on-call coverage, are not checked until the inventory design (#2834) lands. **Upgrade note:** the queries changed, so on the next boot all three bundles are superseded by new versions. Owners, evidence, exceptions and risk links stay with the old versions and have to be moved by hand. Acknowledgements recorded before the upgrade carry no title, so these controls read inconclusive until users acknowledge again or the old records leave the window.
