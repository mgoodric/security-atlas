- **feat(risks): an approver closes a risk import nobody will finish, and the import page lists recent imports with their pending and waiting rows (#3002).** `POST /v1/risks/imports/{id}/close` (or **Close import** on the review page) takes a required reason, rejects every pending row with `import closed: <reason>`, audits each rejection by the closer, and settles the rows of any import waiting on them: they keep waiting while another pending row carries the parent's ID, and fail their parent link otherwise. The batch's status is then `closed`, and it takes no further decision or mapping. The same admin-or-approver gate as approve and reject applies, and the uploader cannot close their own import. `GET /v1/risks/imports` lists the 100 newest imports with `pending_count`, `waiting_count` and `children_waiting_count`, shown under **Risks → Import**. Imports have no expiry. Migration `20261303000000_risk_import_batch_close` adds the `closed` status and the `closed_by`, `closed_at` and `close_reason` columns.
