- **feat(risks): the risk register filters, sorts and pages on the server (#2132).** `GET /v1/risks` takes `status=open|closed|all` (open by default; `include_closed=true` still means all), `owner` (a user id, or `none`), `treatment`, `category`, `methodology`, `level`, `org_unit`, `theme`, `review_due=overdue|due_soon` and an inclusive `review_due_from` / `review_due_to` date range, plus `sort=[-]id|title|category|owner|residual|treatment|review_due|status|methodology|level|org_unit|themes|created_at` and `limit` / `offset` (500 at most). It returns the filtered `total` beside the page. The default order is residual, highest first, with every 5×5 risk above every FAIR risk and unscored risks last. `/risks` asks for the one page it shows; its default columns are ID, Title, Category, Owner, Residual (the band, with inherent severity on hover), Treatment, Review due and Status, and fit at 1316px; a column chooser adds methodology, level, org unit, themes and created. Every header sorts, rows open the risk, and the Status filter replaces "Show closed". The Inherent severity column and the Severity filter are gone.
