- **feat(risks): a risk import places each risk in the organisation's hierarchy (#2845).** `POST /v1/risks/imports` maps a level column (`Level`, `Risk Level`, or `--column-map level=<Header>`; `team`, `org` or `company`, ignoring case) and an org unit column (`Org Unit`, `org_unit`, or `--column-map org_unit=<Header>`), matched by name, ignoring case, to the uploading tenant's org units. The pair follows the create path's placement rule: a unit with no level gives the risk the unit's level, and a row with neither is a team risk. A row is rejected with a reason when its level is not a level (`invalid_level`), its org unit matches none of the tenant's (`unknown_org_unit`) or more than one (`ambiguous_org_unit`), or its level differs from the unit's (`level_mismatch`). A unit deleted or moved to another level after upload is refused at approval and the row stays pending. The review page shows the level and org unit. Migration `20261293000000_risk_import_placement`.
