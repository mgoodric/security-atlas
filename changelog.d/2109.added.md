- **feat(action-plans): plans with linked controls are verified from evidence (#2109).** After each control evaluation, a completed plan whose linked controls all pass their latest evaluation becomes verified, and a verified plan with a linked control that now fails returns to completed. Both moves are the system's, made in the evaluation's transaction and written to the plan's audit log under the system actor `system:action-plan-verification`; no person or key can make them, and the database refuses `verified → completed` outside that path. The plan page and the control and risk pages show "Completed · linked controls still failing" for a completed plan still waiting on its controls, from the new `controls_failing` field on `GET /v1/action-plans/{id}` and the linked-plan lists. Glossary: Completed and Verified (action plan).
