- **feat(calendar): the compliance calendar is the one list of what is due; `GET /v1/upcoming` is removed (#2130).** `GET /v1/calendar` gains three event types: `risk` (a risk's `review_due_at`, roll-ups included, each risk once), `action_plan` (an open plan's `due_date`) and `policy_ack` (a policy acknowledgement lapsing 365 days after the latest ack, per policy version and user). Each carries `overdue` (after the UTC due day), `due-soon` (within 14 days) or `upcoming`. Risk and action-plan events are shown only to a caller OPA lets read risks / action plans (an auditor or control owner sees neither; a viewer sees risk reviews), and the ICS feed, whose token names no user, never carries them. A window that starts in the past returns the items that fell due in it. The dashboard's "Coming up" panel now lists the calendar's next 14 days. **API change:** `GET /v1/upcoming` is gone; read `GET /v1/calendar?from=…&to=…` instead. Decisions: `docs/audit-log/2130-calendar-single-source-decisions.md`.
