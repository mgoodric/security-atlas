- **feat(eval): SQL and Rego evidence queries see the evaluation instant, not the wall clock (#2911, #2890).** A SQL query's `evidence` relation gains a constant `evaluated_at` column, and a Rego policy's `time.now_ns()` is the same instant. It is the evaluation time, or the as-of horizon when a replay or as-of evaluation passes an earlier one, and a bundle test case's `evaluated_at`. So a windowed check ("reported within 24 h") gives a replay the verdict as of its horizon, and a test case with pinned dates keeps passing. Rego's `observed_at` is now UTC. Stock SQL checks may not call `now()`, `current_timestamp`, `clock_timestamp()` or their relatives (a test refuses them); tenant queries should compare against `evaluated_at` too.
