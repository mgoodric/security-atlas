- **Aggregation-rule and mapping-overlay writes refuse a request with no credential id (#2375).** `POST /v1/aggregation-rules`, `PATCH .../activate` and `.../deactivate`, and `POST /v1/mapping-overlays`, `.../{id}/retire` and `.../import` answered by recording the write under a `system` actor when the request carried no credential (or one without an id). They now answer 401 `credential missing` and write nothing, as `PATCH /v1/risks/{id}` does since #1954.
- **Unified audit-log payloads follow the source resource's read (#2375).** On `/v1/admin/audit-log/unified`, `/v1/admin/audit-log/export` and `/v1/activity/unified`, a row whose subject the caller cannot read at its own route (an auditor on aggregation-rule, mapping-overlay, risk-category, feature-flag and authz-decision rows; a viewer or control owner on most kinds; anyone but an admin on another user's me row) now comes back with `payload_json: null`. The row itself (time, actor, kind, target, action) is still returned. Decisions and the role x kind table: `docs/audit-log/2375-unified-log-payload-gate-decisions.md`.
