- **fix(risks): the risk edit form no longer drops unsaved edits when the risk refetches in the background (#2544).** The form keeps the version it opened on until you press Reload, so another editor's save reaches an open form only as the "This risk changed since you opened it" prompt on save (412), never as a silent reset after a tab switch. `PATCH /v1/risks/{id}` now refuses a weak ETag (`W/"…"`) in If-Match with 400, since If-Match compares strongly (RFC 9110), and `GET /v1/risks/{id}` sets `ETag` only on a successful response.
