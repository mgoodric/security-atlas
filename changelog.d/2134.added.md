- **feat(dashboard): `GET /v1/needs-attention`, one ranked list of what needs attention (#2134).** Items come in five bands, in order: failing in-scope controls; approvals waiting for the caller (the same rows as `GET /v1/approvals`); overdue policy, risk and vendor reviews, action plans and periodic control reviews; items due within 14 days, expiring exceptions included; and, in the Everyone view only, in-scope controls and open risks with no owner. Within a band the oldest item comes first. Each item carries its type, title, link, action label, due date or age, and owner. `for=me` (the default) keeps what the caller owns, policies whose owner role they hold as a live role, and their approvals; a caller who has none and may see everyone gets `for=all`. `for=all` is limited to admins and GRC leads (`authz.IsTenantApprover`); anyone else gets 403. The overdue and due-soon bands read the compliance calendar's events with the calendar's read and module filters, so they agree with `/calendar`. Items from modules the caller cannot see are neither listed nor counted. The list is paged (`limit`, default 10, at most 100; `offset`). The attention test fixture's overdue vendor now has an owner, and its overdue policy's owner role is `admin`.
