A new admin-gated endpoint `POST /v1/admin/crosswalk-edges/{id}/tier` transitions a mapping's tier (reusing the slice-509 `cred.IsAdmin` boundary — any admin/maintainer role, NOT super_admin-only; a non-admin caller is 403, the load-bearing threat-model-E mitigation); every transition appends an immutable audit row (reviewer id, from-tier, to-tier, note, timestamp) in the SAME transaction as the tier change (P0-483-4). Nothing auto-promotes an agent-authored `community_draft` — a human admin transition is required (P0-483-1, the constitutional "no auto-approve its own mappings" AI-assist boundary, operationalized for the crosswalk layer).
