- **feat(policies): synced policies are read-only in security-atlas, and the policy page says where to edit them (#2852).** For a policy synced from Confluence (`source_attribution: "synced"`), the API answers 409 "managed in Confluence" to creating its next version, changing its review date, and every draft path: edit, discard, submit, approve and publish, on the synced row and on any draft of its chain. A draft of a synced chain can still be discarded, which frees the slot the page's next version needs. Acknowledgements work as for any other policy. Control links stay in security-atlas, through the new `PATCH /v1/policies/{id}/controls` (`{"linked_control_ids": [...]}`). It replaces a published synced policy's links in place, without a new version. Any other policy gets 409. The change is audit-logged as `controls_updated` with the links before and after, and saving the same set writes nothing. The policy page shows a "Managed in Confluence" badge, the link to the source page, the approval as recorded in Confluence ("Approved in Confluence by <name> on <date>"), and a notice when the page has drifted. It hides the edit and lifecycle actions, and offers a link editor instead. Migration `20261283000000_policy_controls_updated` adds the `controls_updated` audit action and its two link-list columns.
