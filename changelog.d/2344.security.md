- **fix(web): upload BFFs enforce their size cap on the bytes, not only the declared length (#2344).** The five multipart upload routes (control import, control bundle upload, attestation artifact upload, questionnaire Excel import, walkthrough attachment) now read their body through one shared helper, `web/lib/api/upload.ts`. A body with no `Content-Length`, or one that isn't a non-negative integer, gets 411. A body is counted while it streams and cut off with 413 once it passes the route's cap, so a chunked or understated upload can no longer make the web process buffer an unbounded body. The questionnaire import and walkthrough attachment routes had no BFF cap before. They now mirror the platform's 5 MiB and 100 MiB + 1 KiB caps. Browser uploads are unaffected.
