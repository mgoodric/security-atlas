- **feat(risks): linking and unlinking a child risk is in the parent's history (#2958).** `POST /v1/risks/{id}/children` and an applied MCP `link_child_risks` proposal write one `child_linked` row on the parent per new link, by the caller or the confirmer; re-attaching a child that is already linked writes none. `DELETE /v1/risks/{id}/children/{child_id}` and an applied `unlink_child_risk` proposal write one `child_unlinked` row. The rows name the child (`child_risk_id`) and show in the risk's History (by the child's title), the activity feed, and the unified audit log and its export. Migration `20261295000000_risk_audit_hierarchy_links.sql` adds the two actions. Links made by an aggregation rule are not logged here.
