- **Breaking: `GET /v1/features/enabled` is removed; use `GET /v1/me/modules` (#2170, noted in #2339).** The non-admin enabled-modules read from slice 660 was removed by #2317 in favour of `GET /v1/me/modules`, which returns every module with its state (`on`, `switched_off`, `no_access`), its pages and its features. API clients and BFFs that called `/v1/features/enabled` get a 404 and must move to `/v1/me/modules`.
