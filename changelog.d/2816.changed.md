- **fix(controls): the stock security-awareness training control keeps a completion fresh for 400 days, not 90 (#2816).** `soc2_cc1_4_competence_training` declared the `monthly` freshness class (90 days) although it checks for annual training, so a completion went stale three months after it was done. It now declares `annual` (400 days: a year plus 35 days' grace). Tenants who load the updated bundle see training evidence count as fresh for the rest of the training year. Stock quarterly and annual descriptions now state the window the engine applies (120 and 400 days) instead of 90 and 365, and the control-bundle spec documents each freshness class's grace period. The wider window applies to every record, not only passes: **fail** records 91 to 400 days old also come back into scope and count toward the control's evaluation, where before they had aged out. The windows themselves are unchanged.
