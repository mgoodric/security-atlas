- **feat(policies): create, edit, submit, approve, publish and version policies in the app (#2116).** `/policies/new` creates a draft (title, owner and approver roles, linked controls, acknowledgment roles, a Markdown body with preview) and warns before submit that publishing needs a linked control. The policy page carries the workflow in its header: a draft has Edit, Submit for review and Discard; a policy under review has Approve; an approved one has Publish (effective date and next review); a published one has Create new version, which opens a draft linked to it, and publishing that draft supersedes it. The disabled "New policy" placeholder is replaced by a link. New platform route `PATCH /v1/policies/{id}` edits a draft in place and answers 409 once the policy has left draft.
