- **feat(risks): close and reopen a risk (#2107).** A risk is open or closed. Close it from its page with a reason (no longer applicable, merged into another risk, realised and handled) and an optional note; reopen it from the same page. Both are written to the risk audit log. Closed risks leave the register and its count, the heatmaps, the org-unit rollups, the dashboard and board pack top risks, the review calendar and aggregation-rule candidates; the register's "Show closed" (`GET /v1/risks?include_closed=true`) brings them back. The export adds `status` and `closure_reason` columns. Migration `20261209000000_risk_lifecycle.sql` adds the columns; every existing risk is open.
