- **fix(risks): a GRC engineer, and the person who uploaded a risk import, no longer approve or reject its rows (#2881).** Approve-all, per-row approve and reject on `/v1/risks/imports/{id}` now require a person who is a tenant admin or holds the approver flag (`authz.IsRiskImportDecider`), and refuse a `grc_engineer` with 403, though other approver gates still admit that role. The batch's uploader gets 403 on all three for their own batch. Migration `20261296000000_risk_import_uploader.sql` adds `risk_import_batches.uploader_user_id`, filled for existing batches whose `uploaded_by` is a person. `GET /v1/risks/imports/{id}` returns `uploader_user_id` and `can_decide`, and the review page shows the decide controls only when `can_decide` is true. Creating a category or theme from `PUT /v1/risks/imports/{id}/values` stays on the taxonomy gate.
