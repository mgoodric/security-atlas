- **fix(catalog): the stock control hash list is append-only (#2963).** `scripts/gen-stock-control-hashes.sh` rebuilt `internal/api/scfimport/stock_manifests_gen.go` from the history reachable from `HEAD`, so a hash generated from a PR-branch revision that a squash merge discarded was lost on the next regeneration. Three were lost: cc4_1, cc6_1 and cc6_7. They are restored, the generator now keeps every existing entry, and `stock-supersede-check` fails a PR or merge group whose list drops an entry its base holds (`scripts/check-stock-hashes-superset.sh`). `main` never shipped the three revisions, so only installs bootstrapped from those PR branches were affected.
