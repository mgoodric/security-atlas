- **fix(controls): stock controls' `control_family` matches their SCF anchor's family (#2405).** Four stock SOC 2 controls kept their old family after #1979 and #1981 moved their anchors across families. The control detail page, the MCP `controls` tool and the OSCAL statement label showed the stale family. `soc2_cc1_4_competence_training` (`SAT-04`) moves from `HRS` to `SAT`. `soc2_pi1_2_input_completeness`, `soc2_pi1_3_processing_validity` and `soc2_pi1_4_output_delivery` (`TDA-05`) move from `SEA` to `TDA`. A new unit test fails when a stock control's `control_family` differs from the prefix of its `scf_anchor_id`, unless the bundle is on a commented exception list. Three crosswalk rationales now name `HRS-12` by its 2026.3 title, Personnel Termination. **Upgrade note:** a family change supersedes the control, the same as an anchor change. All four controls are already superseded on upgrade by the unreleased #1979 and #1981 changes, so an install upgrading from the last release sees no extra effect.
