- **feat(audit): assign and remove auditors on an audit period (#2118).** An admin opens an audit period and uses its new Auditors card to assign any active auditor in the tenant, or to remove one. Backed by `GET`/`POST /v1/audit-periods/{id}/auditors` and `DELETE /v1/audit-periods/{id}/auditors/{userID}`, admin only. Each change is written to the period's audit log (`auditor_assigned` / `auditor_removed`, migration `20261207000000`) in the same transaction. An assigned auditor gets that one period and nothing more, and a removal takes effect on their next request. Known gap: an auditor signed in through `/login` gets no auditor role until #2239 lands.
