- **fix(policies): sync drift ignores whitespace-only differences (#2910).** `POST /v1/policies:sync` used to compare a page's body with the synced or imported text byte for byte. Because the connector's Confluence-to-Markdown conversion differs in whitespace from an export, most in-place takeovers reported drift on their first run, and kept reporting it until the page's version was bumped. The comparison now normalises both sides first: line endings become `\n`, trailing whitespace is stripped from each line, runs of blank lines collapse to one, and trailing newlines are trimmed. A content change is still drift. Recorded hashes are unchanged and are still of the body as sent. The in-place `sync_takeover` audit row now records the drifted page's hash in a new `sync_drift_body_sha256` column, next to `body_sha256`, which stays the hash of the row's own body. The chained takeover's audit row records the imported policy's `previous_state`/`new_state` (published to superseded). Migration `20261298000000_policy_audit_sync_drift` adds the column.
