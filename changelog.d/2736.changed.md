- **fix(policies): a policy you have never acknowledged becomes overdue 14 days after it takes effect (#2736).** Before, it stayed "due soon" on your needs-attention list forever, so ignoring a policy ranked below letting an acknowledgement lapse. It is now due soon until 14 days after its effective date and overdue after that, due on that date. `GET /v1/me/acknowledgments` reports the same thing on each pending row, as the new `status` (`overdue` or `due_soon`) and `due_at` fields.
