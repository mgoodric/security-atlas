- **feat(risks): a risk import links each risk under the parent its register names (#2846).** `POST /v1/risks/imports` maps a parent-risk-ID column (`Parent Risk ID`, `Parent`, `parent_ref`, or `--column-map parent_ref=<Header>`). The ID is matched by organisation risk ID, ignoring case, against the tenant's risks and the batch's own rows. Links are made after approval, through the same parent/child path as `POST /v1/risks/{id}/children` and under its rules: no cycles, a child strictly below its parent's level, and a 5x5-scored child. A child approved before its parent's row waits, and is linked when that row is approved. When a link breaks a rule, or names a parent that is not a risk and not a pending row of the batch, the failure is recorded on that row (`parent_link_status` `failed`, `parent_link_error`) with the `parent_link_failed` flag, and the risk still imports. Another tenant's risks are never matched. The export's `parent_risk_ids` column holds uuids, so it stays unmapped. The review page shows the parent risk ID and how its link went.
