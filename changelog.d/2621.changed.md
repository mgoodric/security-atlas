- **fix(web): switching tenants resets every browser query cache, not just the people directory (#2621).** Most browser query keys carry no tenant, so after an in-tab switch (or one broadcast from a sibling tab, or a pick on `/oauth/select-tenant`) a user in two tenants could briefly see the previous tenant's titles, counts or lists until the cache went stale. The switch now resets the whole browser cache. **Visible change:** right after a switch, views show their loading state for a moment while they refetch under the new tenant, instead of the old tenant's data.
