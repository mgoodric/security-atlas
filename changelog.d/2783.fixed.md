- **fix(needs-attention): the large-read warning logs once per tenant and source every 15 minutes, to the platform logger (#2783).** A tenant past `ATLAS_NEEDS_ATTENTION_WARN_ROWS` logged up to three WARN lines on every `GET /v1/needs-attention`, and the home page polls that endpoint. Each (tenant, source) pair now warns at most once per `needsattention.WarnInterval` (15 minutes). The router also passes the server's attached logger to the handler, so the warning reaches the logger `cmd/atlas` configures (`Server.AttachLogger`) instead of `slog.Default()`, which `cmd/atlas` never sets.
