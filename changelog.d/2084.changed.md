- **feat(web): one date formatter — relative for recent, absolute otherwise, exact on hover (#2084).** `formatTimestamp` (`web/lib/relative-time.ts`) and the `<Timestamp>` component (`web/components/ui/timestamp.tsx`) replace the per-page formatters and raw ISO strings on Controls (list, evidence stream, evaluation history), Evidence (list and detail), Activity (page and dashboard feed), the audit-period bar, Policies (list and detail), risk detail and the decision timeline. Activity-style stamps within 7 days read relative ("2 days ago"); older ones, and every due, review and period date, read absolute in the viewer's locale and timezone. Calendar days (due dates, period bounds) format in UTC so no timezone moves them to the neighbouring day. The tooltip is the exact instant in UTC, labelled `UTC`. Playwright now pins `locale: en-US` and `timezoneId: UTC`. The remaining pages follow in #2365.
