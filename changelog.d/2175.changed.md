- **feat(modules): background work, MCP proposals and board packs follow module switches (#2175).** The overdue-decision and overdue-offboarding notifications go out only while their module (Risk with its Decision log, or Personnel security) is switched on, and only to people with access to it. A notification held back that way is sent once the module is switched back on or access is widened. The email, Slack and webhook digests leave out notification kinds from a switched-off module, or a module the recipient can't access. Data upkeep, such as recomputing a risk's residual when evidence arrives, keeps running while a module is off. Confirming an MCP write proposal into a switched-off module, such as `create_risk` while Risk is off, is refused with 409 `module_switched_off`. The proposal stays pending and applies once the module is back on. A new board pack leaves out the sections of a switched-off module (vendor burndown for Third-party risk, top risks for Risk) and lists them in `omitted_sections`. The pack can be published without them. Packs generated earlier keep every section they had.
