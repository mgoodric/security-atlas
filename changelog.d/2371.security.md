- **fix(risks): a risk acceptance is approved only through the approval rule (#2371).** `POST /v1/risks` and the MCP `create_risk` tool used to accept `treatment=accept` with `acceptance_status=approved` and a free-text `accepter`. That recorded an approval with no separation of duties. Both now return 400 (`treatment_validation_failed`) for `acceptance_status=approved`, `accepter` or `accepted_until` at create. A risk created with `acceptance_status=proposed` records its creator as the proposer, so the creator cannot approve it unless the organisation allows self-approval. Approving an acceptance with no recorded proposer (proposed before #2105) now returns 409 for everyone; reject it and propose it again. Through MCP, the person who confirms a proposal is recorded as the proposer and the actor, as REST records them. Clients that sent an approved acceptance on create must now approve it in a second call. The counts of existing affected risks come from the read-only queries in `docs/audit-log/2371-risk-create-acceptance-decisions.md`; no data is changed.
