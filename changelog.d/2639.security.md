- **fix(actionplan): the verify owner rule no longer depends on audit-row order (#2639).** `action_plan_audit_log.created_at` now defaults to `clock_timestamp()`, the time of the write, not the transaction start. An owner change that waited on the plan's row lock behind a completion now sorts after it. The rule also counts the owner on any audit row whose before- or after-status is `completed` or `verified`, wherever that row sorts. Existing rows keep their timestamps.
