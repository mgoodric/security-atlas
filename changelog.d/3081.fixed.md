- **fix(audit): a frozen audit period can no longer be deleted (#3081).** `atlas_app` holds DELETE on `audit_periods`, and only UPDATE of a frozen period was refused in the database, so a frozen period with no populations, notes, walkthroughs or freeze revisions could be deleted, erasing its `frozen_hash` and the control set its freeze recorded. A `BEFORE DELETE` trigger now refuses a frozen period for every role, the owner included. Open periods can still be deleted. Migration `20261313000000_audit_period_frozen_no_delete` (with down file). The demo teardown disables the trigger around its own `audit_periods` DELETE as the owner, in its transaction; manual teardown follows `docs/runbooks/audit-period-reopen.md`. The unbound-rows purge now keeps all-zeros-tenant audit periods instead of deleting them.
