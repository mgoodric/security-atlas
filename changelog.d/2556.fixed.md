- **fix(authz): viewers can read the risk register and the decision log, and still write neither (#2556).** `viewer.rego` now admits viewers to `decisions` reads. Before this, a viewer got 403 on `/v1/decisions` and everything under it. The handler read gates on the risk list, theme heatmap, risk export, decision list and org-unit list now check the caller's live tenant role (`authz.IsTenantRiskReader`: admin, grc_engineer or viewer) and no longer trust the session's role claim. That claim stays valid after a role is revoked, until the token expires. Control owners and auditors were already refused by OPA on these routes, and they still are.
