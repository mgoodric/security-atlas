- **feat(crosswalk): carried-edge sync reports why it held an edge back, and reports rejected copies it rewrote (#3079).** Upgrade note: when a crosswalk's content replaces an SCF-remap carried edge's (#3073), the edge's tier re-seeds from the new content's source (ADR 0018 "Tier follows content"). A copy that inherited `verified` from its source drops to `draft` when a non-`scf_official` row rewrites it, and goes back to a reviewer; a `draft` copy rewritten by an `scf_official` row seeds at `verified`. A `rejected` copy keeps its tier, and a rewrite that changes its provenance is now listed with the other upgraded-onto-rejected edges. `import-crosswalk` and `converge-crosswalks` print `reason=human_reviewed` or `reason=outranked` on each carried edge they keep.
