- **feat(risks): a risk import batch declares one scoring methodology, and staged risks arrive scored from the register's columns (#2841).** `POST /v1/risks/imports` takes a `methodology` field (`atlas-cli risks import --methodology`): `nist_800_30` (the default when omitted), `qualitative_5x5` or `fair`. It is stored on the batch and shown on it, and every risk the batch creates gets that methodology. Inherent and residual scores are read from columns in the methodology's own shape. For `nist_800_30` and `qualitative_5x5` that is inherent and residual likelihood and impact, whole numbers from 1 to 5. For `fair` it is loss event frequency and loss magnitude, plain non-negative numbers. Headers such as "Likelihood", "Residual Impact", "LEF" and "Residual LM" are recognised, and `--column-map` names any other spelling. A row with no score cells imports unscored. A row with half a pair, a value out of range, or a residual with no inherent score is rejected with the new `invalid_score` reason, which says what was wrong. The score columns of another methodology are kept as unmapped. Migration `20261286000000_risk_import_scores`.
