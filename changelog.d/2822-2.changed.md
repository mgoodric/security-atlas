- **feat(controls): stock control cc4_1 checks that every reporting host checked in within 24 hours, in SQL over the osquery host-posture payload (#2822, #1860).** `soc2_cc4_1_continuous_monitoring` used to roll up the osquery connector's verdict, which is disk encryption plus screen lock. It now takes each host's latest in-window `osquery.host_posture.v1` record and compares the host's `last_seen_at` (Fleet's `seen_time`) with the evaluation instant, so a replay is measured from its as-of time. A host last seen more than 24 hours earlier fails the control; the control passes when every host was seen within 24 hours and is inconclusive when any host's latest record has no readable `last_seen_at` (missing, malformed, or more than an hour after the evaluation instant) or there is no record. Records without a `host_uuid` are left out. Only hosts that sent records are checked; coverage of every managed endpoint waits on an endpoint inventory (#2834). The title is now "Endpoints reporting posture have checked in within 24 hours", and the bundle ships `tests/` cases. **Upgrade note:** the query changed, so on the next boot the bundle is superseded by a new version. Owners, evidence, exceptions and risk links stay with the old version and have to be moved by hand. Records from a connector older than schema 1.1.0 carry no `last_seen_at` and leave the control inconclusive.
