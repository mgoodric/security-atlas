- **fix(web): after a confirmed delete, focus moves to a neighbouring row instead of dropping to the page (#2682).** Deleting a taxonomy category or theme, revoking a session, unlinking a control from a risk, or detaching a child risk removes the row, and its trigger with it. Keyboard focus used to fall to `<body>`. Now it moves to the next row's trigger. If there is no next row it goes to the previous row's trigger, skipping any that are disabled, and if no row can take it, to the list heading. `ConfirmInline` also stops treating a later Confirm as a cancel when it was kept mounted after one.
