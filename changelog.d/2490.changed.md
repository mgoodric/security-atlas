- **feat(risks): the platform counts the sidebar's high-severity risks, and a non-numeric score is refused on write (#2490).** `GET /v1/risks` takes `?severity_band=high|medium|low`: the open risks whose assessed 5x5 severity (the wire `severity`) is in that band, FAIR left out, with the filtered `total`. The sidebar badge reads that total off a one-row page instead of downloading the whole register every minute, and the register's "N of M" reads its own one-row total under its own query key. The whole-list risk pickers stop after 50 pages and flag the result `partial` rather than looping. `POST /v1/risks` returns 422 for a non-FAIR `score`, `severity`, `likelihood` or `impact` that is not a JSON number in range (0..25, or 1..5 for likelihood and impact), in either score, and for a non-empty `residual_score` that carries none of them; `PATCH /v1/risks/{id}` returns 422 for such an `inherent_score`. FAIR keeps its own validation. `scripts/dry-run-2490-risk-score-numbers.sql` counts, read-only, the stored risks the new rule would refuse.
