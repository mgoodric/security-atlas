- **feat(risks): every risk hierarchy link and unlink is in History, including rule-made links, deletes, and the child's own History (#2961).** An aggregation rule writes a `child_linked` row on its meta-risk for each child it newly links and a `child_unlinked` row for each child it drops, by `system:aggregation-rule:<rule_id>`; a re-evaluation that keeps the same children writes none. `POST /v1/risks/aggregate` writes a `child_linked` row per child by the caller. Deleting a risk drops its links in the delete's transaction and writes a `child_unlinked` row for each one, on the parent's trail, by the deleting admin, with a note naming the delete. A child's History (`GET /v1/risks/{id}/audit-log`) now also lists the link and unlink rows of its parents, each marked with `parent_risk_id`, and reads "Linked under a parent risk" / "Unlinked from a parent risk" with the parent's title. Migration `20261300000000_risk_audit_child_link_index.sql` adds a partial index for that read.
