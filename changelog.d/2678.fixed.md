- **fix(evidence): a rate-limited `POST /v1/evidence:push` never answers `Retry-After: 0` (#2678).** The header now rounds the wait up to whole seconds, minimum 1, like the other rate-limited endpoints. It used to round to the nearest second, so a wait under half a second advertised 0 and told clients to retry at once; a client that honours the header is now admitted on its retry.
