- **feat(admin): organisation admins issue and revoke their own SCIM credentials (#2503).** A tenant admin by its live roles can now call `POST/GET /v1/admin/scim-credentials` and `DELETE /v1/admin/scim-credentials/{id}` for its own tenant. The token it mints provisions only that tenant through `/scim/v2`. Another tenant's credential id is a 404.
