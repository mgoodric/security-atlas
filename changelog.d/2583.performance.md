- **perf(web): walkthrough attachment and artifact uploads stream through the BFF instead of being buffered (#2583).** `POST /api/audit/walkthroughs/{id}/attachments` (100 MiB cap) and `POST /api/artifacts/upload` (10 MiB cap) now forward the body to the platform as it arrives, through a counting stream that pulls one client chunk only when the platform reads one, so the web process holds about one chunk per upload rather than two to three copies of the whole body. The caps are unchanged: a missing or malformed Content-Length is 411, a body past the cap is 413 even when the header understates it. A client stream that breaks mid-upload now answers 400 "upload interrupted" on every upload route, not the "not multipart" 400; an unreachable platform is 502. The walkthrough route no longer parses the multipart itself, so a malformed body gets the platform's 400. `just fmt-frontend` now runs the pre-commit prettier hook, the repo's single prettier pin, instead of a no-op.
