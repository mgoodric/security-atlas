- **fix(evidence,eval): the freeze horizon, live evaluation and `ingested_at` use one clock; live ingest waits for or retries around a freeze (#3038).** Live evaluation now reads `evaluated_at` from the database clock, the clock that stamps `ingested_at`, so a row ingested just before T is counted by the live result at T and by a replay of T even when the app host's clock drifts from the database's. Every ledger insert now takes the tenant evidence-freeze lock in shared mode, so a freeze waits for in-flight ingests. An ingest whose transaction began before a freeze that has since committed is refused: a push retries it in a new transaction, and a domain writer's request (change, incident, access review, and the rest) fails without writing anything, and resubmitting it succeeds. Stored freeze hashes are unchanged; ADR 0003 names the two cases where an external recompute of a period frozen before this change can differ.
