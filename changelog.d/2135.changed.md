- **feat(dashboard): the dashboard leads with what needs you (#2135).**
  - **Order.** Four sections, in order: **Needs attention** (the top 10 from `GET /v1/needs-attention`), **Posture** (framework tiles plus a top-risks card), **Coming up** (the calendar's next 14 days) and **Recent activity** (the last 10 activity rows as sentences). Evidence freshness, drift and the portfolio summary follow, unchanged.
  - **Needs attention.** Each row shows its type, band, owner and the action to take. Admins and GRC leads get a For you / Everyone toggle; a user who owns nothing and may see everyone lands on Everyone. **See all** opens the new `/needs-attention` page, which pages 25 at a time and filters by type.
  - **Top risks.** The card lists the 5 open risks with the highest residual, roll-ups left out, in the register's residual order, with band, owner and treatment. It used to list risks in treatment `mitigate` only.
  - **Audit banner.** The top-bar pill reads "Audit ends in N days", counted to the open period's end date in the viewer's timezone, and links to the period.
  - **API.** `GET /v1/needs-attention` takes `?type=` (comma-separated item types) and returns `type_counts`. `GET /v1/activity/unified` takes `?limit=` (1 to 1000).
