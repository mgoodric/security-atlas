- **feat(risks): import a risk register from CSV or XLSX, staged and approved by a person (#2839).** `POST /v1/risks/imports` (or `atlas-cli risks import <file> --column-map field=Header`) stages a register: each row is either staged with its organisation risk ID, title, description, category and review due, or rejected with a reason. Columns no field takes are kept on the staged row, never in the description. A tenant approver who is a person approves the pending rows with `POST /v1/risks/imports/{id}/approve`, and each becomes a risk through the risk store with a `created` audit row naming them. A row whose risk ID the tenant already uses is marked duplicate, and re-importing never updates a risk. Uploads are capped at 5 MiB, 5000 rows and 200 columns, and each decision writes a `risk_import_audit_log` row. Migration `20261282000000_risk_import_batches`.
