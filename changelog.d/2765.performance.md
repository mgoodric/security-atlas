- **perf(controls): the `/controls` state list and its total find each control's latest evaluation by index instead of reading the whole evaluation ledger (#2765).** `ListActiveControlsWithState` and `CountActiveControls` used a `DISTINCT ON` over every `control_evaluations` row in the tenant. They now skip-scan the existing `idx_control_evaluations_latest` index, one probe per control and scope cell. On a tenant with 211,200 evaluations the list page went from 96 ms to 5 ms, and the total with a state filter from 10.4 s to 8 ms. Results are unchanged, and needs-attention, which reads the same list, benefits too. No migration.
