- **feat(controls): cc6_8 fails a host whose anti-malware definitions are more than 7 days old; osquery reads the date from a named Fleet report (#2936).** `osquery.host_posture.v1` 1.4.0 adds `antimalware_definitions_updated_at` with `antimalware_definitions_source` (`fleet_report:<name>`). The osquery connector pushes 1.4.0 and fills the date from one column of a Fleet report named with `--antimalware-definitions-report` and `--antimalware-definitions-column` (RFC 3339 or Unix epoch seconds; with several rows, the oldest). No osquery table or Fleet host field reports a definitions date, so the date is only as good as the operator's query, and none was verified against a live host. `soc2_cc6_8_malware_protection` now fails a host whose latest date is more than 168 hours before the evaluation instant. A host with no date, or a malformed or future one, is judged on `antimalware_protected` alone, as before. **Upgrade note:** on the first start after upgrading, `soc2_cc6_8_malware_protection` is superseded by a new version. Re-assign its owner and move exceptions and risk links to the new version. Without the new flags, records carry no date and verdicts do not change.
