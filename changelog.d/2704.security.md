- **fix(web): the BFF forwarders no longer relay a platform 5xx body, and an upload proxy call ends when its client disconnects (#2704).** `forwardJSON`, `forwardMultipart` and `proxyBoundedUpload` keep the platform's status (and `Retry-After`) but replace any 5xx body with `{"error":"platform error"}`, so a handler that writes an internal error on a 5xx cannot put it on the wire. A 4xx refusal still passes through in the platform's words. `proxyBoundedUpload` passes the request's abort signal to the platform fetch, so a walkthrough attachment or artifact upload whose client goes away no longer leaves the BFF waiting on the platform.
