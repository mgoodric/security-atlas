- **feat(policies): a policy sync can mark a page `unreadable` instead of leaving it out, so its policy is not reported missing (#3000).** A page entry in `POST /v1/policies:sync` may carry `unreadable: true` and an `unreadable_reason` in place of `body_md` and `properties`. The page counts as present, which clears `sync_missing_since`. Nothing else about its policy changes: no body, hash, property, drift or version is written. The run report gives it status `unreadable` with an `unreadable_since` time and adds an `unreadable` count. The policy API returns `sync_unreadable` (`since`, `reason`) on `GET /v1/policies/{id}` and `GET /v1/policies`. `GET /v1/needs-attention` lists the policy in a new `source_unreadable` band ("Unreadable at source"), ranked after `source_missing`. A page that has no policy yet gets none. The reason is cut to 500 characters, and its control characters are replaced. The Confluence connector now sends pages its converter refuses as `unreadable` instead of leaving them out. Its report prints the platform's `unreadable` line, and its totals end in `unreadable=N` in place of `skipped_by_connector=N`. `--max-skipped` and exit 3 still count those pages. Migration `20261302000000_policy_sync_unreadable` adds the two columns, allowed only on synced rows.
