- **feat(needs-attention): a policy waiting for your acknowledgement is on your needs-attention list (#2587).** `GET /v1/needs-attention` gains the `policy_ack` item type: a published policy aimed at a role you hold live, or at everyone, that you have not acknowledged in the last 365 days. A lapsed acknowledgement is overdue from the day it lapsed; one never made is due soon. The item links to the policy and is yours in the for-me view. The admin flag does not put every policy on an admin's list, and there is no item while Policies or its acknowledgements feature is switched off. `?type=policy_ack` filters to them, and the full page offers the filter.
