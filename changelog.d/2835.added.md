- **feat(risks): a risk's creation and its theme changes are in its history (#2835).** Every way a risk is made (`POST /v1/risks`, `POST /v1/risks/aggregate`, an applied MCP `create_risk` proposal, and an aggregation rule's meta-risk) writes a `created` row to the risk's audit log. The row names who made it and the initial title, category, treatment and owner. A rule's meta-risk names the rule (`system:aggregation-rule:<id>`). Assigning or removing a theme writes `themes_changed`. Both show in the risk's History and in the unified audit log, which names the fields only. Migration `20261275000000_risk_audit_created.sql` adds the two actions. `POST /v1/risks`, `POST /v1/risks/aggregate` and the theme routes now answer 401 without a credential.
