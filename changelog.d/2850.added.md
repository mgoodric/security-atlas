- **feat(policies): `POST /v1/policies:sync` turns new Confluence pages into published policies (#2850).** The Confluence policy connector (#2813) pushes the full set of a space's policy pages, with a `policy-sync` key. Only that kind of key is accepted. Admin keys, ordinary keys and user sessions are refused. Each new page becomes a `published` policy whose `source_attribution` is `synced`. The policy keeps the page's approver and approval date as its historical approver, the page URL as its source URL, and a hash of its body. Every create writes a `synced` policy audit row naming the key. Page properties are validated with the policy import's rules, including the audience enum. A page with a missing or invalid property is skipped with a reason, and the pages beside it still sync. Re-pushing an unchanged page writes nothing. The response is a per-page report: created, unchanged, or skipped with a reason. Synced policies show up in the list, the calendar, needs-attention and acknowledgement targeting, as any published policy does. For now a page whose version or body has changed is reported as skipped and its published copy stays as it is. Version chaining and drift come in #2851.
