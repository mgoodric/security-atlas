- **feat(controls): Evaluate now, Download bundle and Upload new version on the control page (#2110).** `POST /v1/controls/{id}/evaluate` runs evaluation for one control synchronously, through the same `EvaluateControl` the scheduler and ingest use (trigger `manual`), and returns its new state. A tenant admin or the control's assigned owner may call it; concurrent calls for one control share one run, bounded at 30 s. `GET /v1/controls/{id}/bundle` downloads the control's stored `control.yaml` as a tarball the bundle upload reads back. The control page's header gains Evaluate now (showing "Evaluating…" while it runs), Download bundle, and an Upload new version form over the existing `POST /v1/controls:upload-bundle`.
