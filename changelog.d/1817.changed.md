- **feat(web): the risk form and register use the organisation's risk categories (#1817, part of #1745).** The `/risks/new` category picker lists the organisation's active categories by name, opens on the organisation default, and posts the slug, so renaming a category never breaks an open form. A category the platform still refuses (one added before the `risk_category` enum is retired) shows the backend's 400 in the form's error alert. The register's Category filter lists the organisation's categories; a new "Show archived categories" toggle (`?archived=1`) adds archived ones, alongside the roll-ups toggle. A risk on an archived category shows the category by name with an "archived" mark in the register and on the risk detail. New BFF route `GET /api/risks/categories` forwards `include_archived`.
