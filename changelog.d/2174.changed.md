- **feat(modules): links, ownership and pickers follow module access (#2174).** A record's link sections into a module the viewer cannot see are left out entirely: on a control page, the Policies tab and linked-policies card disappear without Policies access, and the Risks tab, risks card and linked action plans disappear without Risk. API responses leave those links out too: an incident's risk and vendor links, and a decision's exception links. An owner who loses access to a module keeps their vendors, risks and action plans; anyone who can see the record now gets an "Owner can't access this module" flag (`owner_lacks_access`), worked out from the owner's live access each time it is read. Owner pickers on vendors, risks and action plans list only members who can work in that module, filtered on the server through `GET /v1/people?module=<key>`.
