- **fix(authz): an auditor's per-control evidence list is bounded by the assigned population's window (#2424).** An assigned auditor's `GET /v1/evidence?control_id=…` could previously pass any `since`/`until` and list years of that control's evidence metadata. The list is now admitted only with explicit `since` and `until` that both fall inside a covering whole-control population's time window and not after its `frozen_at`; a missing, malformed, partly overlapping or post-freeze range is refused. Other roles' evidence lists are unchanged.
