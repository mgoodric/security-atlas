- **feat(catalog): the bundled SOC 2 crosswalk covers the Privacy criteria P1.1-P8.1 (#1753).** `data/crosswalks/soc2-tsc-2017.yaml` now has all 61 Trust Services Criteria. Each of the 18 Privacy criteria has a title and a one-line body written in our own words (no AICPA text is quoted) and at least one SCF anchor, mostly in the PRI family, with IRO-22 (Data Breach) for the breach criteria. The SCF sample gains those 17 anchors, titles verbatim from SCF 2026.3. **Upgrade note:** on the next boot the 18 criteria and their edges are added to the existing frozen `soc2` version `2017`; the 43 stored criteria are unchanged and no new framework version is created. **SOC 2 coverage changes:** `requirement_count` for SOC 2 2017 goes from 43 to 61, and SOC 2 coverage is now measured against all 61 criteria. No stock control covers a Privacy criterion yet (a Privacy control kit is a follow-up). Until one exists, or your own controls cover them, the 18 new criteria count as uncovered. For example, a program that covered all 43 criteria before the upgrade now shows 43 of 61 (about 70%). A program whose SOC 2 report excludes Privacy should mark P1.1-P8.1 `excluded` in its SOC 2 framework scope's Statement of Applicability, which takes them out of the denominator and restores its earlier figure. Expect the board pack's SOC 2 coverage and its 90-day trend to show the drop.
