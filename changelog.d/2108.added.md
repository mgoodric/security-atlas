- **feat(action-plans): move a plan through its lifecycle and edit it from its page (#2108).** The plan page has the shared header: Start, Block, Unblock, Mark complete and Reopen, each confirmed inline and toasted, and an Edit route for title, description, owner (picked by name) and due date. A completed plan with no linked controls can be verified by an approver through the new `POST /v1/action-plans/{id}/verify`; the plan's owner never can, even with self-approval allowed, and a plan with linked controls is refused (409). `PATCH /v1/action-plans/{id}` no longer accepts `status: "verified"` (422). An owner must now be an active user of the tenant.
