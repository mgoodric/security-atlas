- **fix(eval, audit): a replay of a past date, and a frozen audit period, count only evidence that was in the ledger at that instant (#1699).** Evidence with a backdated `observed_at` (a late connector push, a backdated upload, a clock-skewed source) used to join a replay of any date after its `observed_at`, and a frozen period's control state, sample population and `frozen_hash` recompute, even though it was written to the ledger later. Every as-of and freeze-horizon read of `evidence_records` now bounds `ingested_at` by the same horizon as `observed_at`: `ListEvidenceForControlAsOf`, `ListEvidenceIDsForPeriodHash`, `ListEvidenceForPeriodControl`, `CountPopulationEvidence`, `ListPopulationEvidenceIDs` and the two `*BeforeHorizon` frozen-summary reads. A replay also measures the freshness window from the replayed instant instead of the wall clock, so replaying a date more than one window old no longer marks its evidence stale. Live evaluation is unchanged. No migration.
