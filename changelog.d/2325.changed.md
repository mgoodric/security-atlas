- **feat(catalog): SCF remap proposals page by cursor and drop stale rows (#2325).** `GET /v1/controls/remap-proposals` takes `limit` (default 100, max 500) and `cursor`, and returns `total` and `next_cursor`. A `class_not` filter lists rebound controls that still deserve a look (`?status=rebound&class_not=unchanged`). Open or rejected proposals whose control has a newer version are left out. A re-run of `catalog import-scf` no longer counts existing proposals again in its `remap_proposals` summary. The review page reads every page, and `atlas-cli catalog remap list` gains `--class-not`, `--limit` and `--cursor`.
