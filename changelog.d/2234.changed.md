- **fix(calendar): group lapsing policy acknowledgements, drop dead ones, and filter the export's upcoming panel like the calendar (#2234, follow-up to #2130).** `policy_ack` events are now one per policy and lapse day, titled `<policy>: N acknowledgements lapse on <date>`, so an ack campaign no longer fills the 500-event cap or "Coming up". Acks of a superseded policy version and acks by a disabled or SCIM-deactivated user are left out. The dashboard export's upcoming panel now lists the event types the caller may read on `GET /v1/calendar`, from the same OPA check. **API change:** a `policy_ack` event's `id` is `<policy_version_id>:<YYYY-MM-DD>` and its `related_entity_kind`/`related_entity_id` are `policy` and the policy version id (they were the acknowledgement). Subscriber-bound ICS tokens are deferred to #2331.
