- **feat(policies): a page's first sync takes over the policy imported for it (#2854).** An adopter who ran the one-time policy import before turning on Confluence sync now ends up with one history per policy. On a page's first sync, `POST /v1/policies:sync` looks for a published, imported policy in the tenant with the page's title. With the same version, that policy becomes the synced policy in place: its acknowledgements and control links are kept, its source link points at the page, and a page body that differs from the imported text is reported as drift. With a different version, the synced version is published as its next version and supersedes it. A policy authored in security-atlas with the same title is never taken over; the page starts a new policy and the report says so. The run report gains a `taken_over` outcome and count. Each takeover writes a `sync_takeover` policy audit row naming the previous attribution and the sync key. Migration `20261288000000_policy_sync_takeover` adds the action and the `previous_source_attribution` column.
