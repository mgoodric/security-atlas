- **fix(controls): the UCF graph sizes CJK, emoji and uppercase-heavy labels to fit their nodes (#2374).** Label widths are now estimated per grapheme, with CJK, fullwidth and emoji glyphs counted at a full em and uppercase Latin counted wider than the average glyph. Labels in those scripts no longer spill past a node's edge, and a wrapped label never splits a combining mark or an emoji sequence. The graph SVG also has a `<title>`, and Playwright specs are now typechecked by `npm run typecheck`.
