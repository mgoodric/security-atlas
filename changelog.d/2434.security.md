- **fix(action-plans): verifying a plan checks its audit trail, not only its current owner (#2434).** Whoever moved the plan to completed, and anyone who owned it from completion onwards, can no longer verify it, so handing a plan to a colleague, verifying, and taking it back is refused. The check fails closed when the trail cannot be read. Plan edits and verification now lock the plan row, so a control link or owner change made at the same moment is seen before the plan is verified.
