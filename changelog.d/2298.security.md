- **fix(auth): the OIDC login state expires on the server and its key is derived with HKDF (#2298).** The signed `state` now carries its issue time under the MAC, and the callback refuses a state older than 10 minutes (30 s clock skew) or dated in the future, so a captured flow cannot be replayed after the browser would have dropped its cookies. The state key derived from `BEARER_HASH_KEY` now uses HKDF-SHA256 and can no longer equal a bearer-token hash. Upgrade note: OIDC logins in progress at deploy time fail once and must be restarted.
