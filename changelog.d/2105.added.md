- **feat(risks): change a risk's treatment and propose, approve, reject or revoke its acceptance from the risk page (#2105).** New routes `PATCH /v1/risks/{id}/treatment` and `PATCH /v1/risks/{id}/acceptance/{propose,approve,reject,revoke}`. Each change is written to the risk's History. Approving needs an approver and an `accepted_until`. The proposer cannot approve their own acceptance unless the organisation allows self-approval, which is then marked Self-approved. Rejecting or revoking returns the risk to untreated. A risk with an acceptance proposed or approved changes treatment only after it is rejected or revoked. Confirmed MCP `update_risk_treatment` proposals go through the same steps, with the confirming person as proposer or approver. The tool now takes a `justification` and no longer takes an `accepter`.
