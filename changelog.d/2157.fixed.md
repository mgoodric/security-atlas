- **fix(authz): an assigned auditor can read their period's populations, samples and evidence, and draw and annotate samples (#2157).** The auditor rules compare the caller's assigned periods with the resource's audit period, but nothing put the resource's period on the decision input, so every one of those requests was refused. The authz middleware now looks it up from the route: a population's period, a sample's population's period, and, for an evidence record, the periods whose attached populations contain it (same control, inside the window and freeze horizon, in a selected scope cell). An auditor reads an evidence list only for one control, and only when an assigned period's population covers the whole control; there is no tenant-wide evidence read. Unassigned periods, unselected scope cells and unattached populations stay refused. Admin, grc_engineer, control_owner and viewer access is unchanged.
