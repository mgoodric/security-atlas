- **fix(risk): the residual subscriber no longer redelivers a deterministic derive failure forever, and one bad risk no longer blocks the rest (#2964).** A risk whose stored `inherent_score` cannot yield a residual (a malformed non-empty score, or a methodology with no scalar score) now fails with `risk.ErrUnderivable`. The subscriber recomputes every other risk linked to the same control, logs the underivable one, counts it on `atlas.risk.residual_underivable`, and terms the message instead of NAK-ing it under `MaxDeliver: -1`. Transient DB and connection errors still NAK, after the remaining risks have been recomputed.
