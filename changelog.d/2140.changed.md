- **feat(controls): the control page leads with a state strip and reads in three sections (#2140).**
  - **State strip.** Under the shared header: current state, last evaluated, freshness (the stalest scope cell), owner, owner role, and markers for an exception covering the control and an open action plan.
  - **Sections.** Then **Evidence** (each row opens the record's detail), **Coverage** (the frameworks and requirements reached, with org mappings, the Statement of Applicability exclusions and a "Covered by exception" marker, and the effective scope per framework) and **Linked** (risks, exceptions, policies and action plans). Exceptions appear on the control page for the first time. A section whose read fails shows its own error; the rest still renders.
  - **Framework map.** The graph moved to its own tab, `?tab=map`. The tabs are now Overview, Framework map and History; links to the retired Evidence, Mappings, Effective scope, Policies and Risks tabs open the overview, which holds that content.
  - **AI explanation.** The gap explanation and the evidence summary show only when one is available, with no "unavailable" notice.
  - **Who sees what.** Upload new version and Request exception show to admins and GRC engineers, Evaluate now and Attest also to control owners, all from the live `/v1/me`. A role the platform refuses the exceptions read (viewer, control owner) sees no Exceptions part. Exceptions and action plans are left out when their module is off.
  - **API.** `GET /v1/controls/{id}/coverage` returns `exclusion` on each requirement the activated framework scope excludes.
