It now reads tickets labelled `vulnerability` and fails when one misses its SLA: critical 168 hours, high 720, medium 2,160, measured to `resolved_at` for a resolved ticket (inclusive) and to the evaluation instant for an open one, which must be younger than its SLA. A ticket with no severity passes only within 168 hours and is otherwise inconclusive. `soc2_cc1_4_competence_training`'s Jira query was the same roll-up. It now reads tickets labelled `security-training` and fails when an assignee has no ticket resolved Done within 365 days and no open one younger than 30 days. **Upgrade note:** both queries changed, so on the next boot both stock bundles are superseded by new versions.
