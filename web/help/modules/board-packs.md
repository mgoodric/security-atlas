A board pack is the quarterly report you take to your board. It turns the
programme's live data into eight fixed sections: posture summary, top
risks and how long they have been open, coverage trend, open findings,
vendor risk burndown, operational metrics, investment against coverage,
and asks of the board.

## From draft to published

1. **Generate draft** with the quarter-end date. The pack is filled from
   your data as of that date. Its narrative comes from fixed templates;
   no AI writes any of it.
2. Review each section. Edit its text, and type in the figures
   security-atlas can't collect itself, such as spend, phishing test pass
   rate or incident count. **Save section** keeps your changes.
3. **Approve section** for each one. A section you haven't approved
   stops the pack being published, and the page lists which are left.
4. **Approve and publish** with the approver's name. Publishing freezes
   the pack at that moment: it can't be edited again, so the board's copy
   and yours always match.

Use **Export PDF** or **Copy Markdown** to take the pack into your board
papers.

## When it is available

Board reporting is an optional module and is off until an admin switches
it on under Admin, then Features.

## Who can do this

Editing, approving and publishing need the approver right; anyone else
sees a read-only view. Admins and GRC engineers can open the packs.
