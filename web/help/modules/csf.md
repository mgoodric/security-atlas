The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) is assessed differently from
frameworks such as SOC 2. Rather than asking whether each requirement is
met, it asks two questions: how mature is the programme as a whole, and
how far is it from where you want to be? This page answers both for the
CSF version you have loaded.

## What this page shows

- **Tier rating**: how rigorous and risk-informed the programme is, from
  Tier 1, Partial, to Tier 4, Adaptive, with the rationale recorded for
  it. "Not yet rated" means nobody has recorded a tier.
- **Current vs Target gap**: one row per CSF outcome (a Subcategory, such
  as GV.OC-01) in either profile, with its current level, its target
  level, and the gap. Levels run from not targeted through partial and
  largely to fully. A row shows **Met** when the current level reaches the
  target, or **+N** for how many steps are still to go.

A positive gap is work to plan, not a failure: the target profile is your
own choice of where the programme should be.

## When it appears

CSF assessment is listed in the sidebar only once a NIST CSF framework
version is loaded, and opens that version.

## Who can do this

Admins and GRC engineers can open the assessment. The page shows the tier
and profiles; it doesn't edit them yet.
