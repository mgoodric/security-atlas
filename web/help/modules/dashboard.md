The dashboard is where a working day starts. It answers three questions
in order: what needs me now, how is the program doing, and what is
coming. Nothing here is a separate record: each section reads the
controls, risks, policies, vendors and approvals you already keep.

## What this page shows

- **Needs attention** lists the most urgent work first: failing
  controls, records waiting for your approval, overdue reviews and due
  dates, then work due in the next two weeks. **For you** keeps what you
  own and what waits for your approval. **Everyone** adds the rest of the
  organisation, plus controls and open risks that have no owner yet.
  **See all** opens the full list, which you can filter by type.
- **Posture** shows each framework's coverage and your five highest open
  risks by residual risk, with their owners and treatments. When you
  activate a scope on a new framework version, the previous version's
  posture target comes with it unless the new version already has one.
- **Coming up** shows the next 14 days of the compliance calendar.
- **Recent activity** shows the last ten things that happened, as
  sentences.

Below these come evidence freshness, recent drift (controls whose state
changed) and an AI-assisted summary of current evidence. The summary
cites the evidence it draws on. It is a reading aid only, never an audit
artifact, and nothing on this page publishes or approves anything.

While an audit period is open, the top bar shows how many days are left
and links to the audit.

## Who can do what

- **See the dashboard**: everyone who can sign in. Each section shows
  only the areas your role can read.
- **Switch to Everyone**, and **set a framework's posture target** from
  its tile: admins and GRC engineers. Everyone else sees For you.
- Acting on an item happens on its own page, under that page's rules.
