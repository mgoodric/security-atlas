People joining and leaving are one of the commonest causes of security
gaps: an account left active after someone leaves, or access granted
without anyone approving it. Auditors ask you to show that every joiner
and leaver was handled. Personnel security keeps a checklist for each
one.

## Checklists

A checklist is either onboarding or offboarding. Onboarding covers
manager approval, granting access and attaching evidence of it, and is
due within 7 days. Offboarding covers confirming the departure,
revoking access, ending sessions and attaching evidence of the
revocation, and is due within 24 hours.

A checklist can be started by hand with **New checklist**, or created
automatically when your HR system (Rippling, BambooHR or SCIM) reports
someone starting or leaving. Nothing here grants or removes access
itself: the checklist records that a person did it.

Completing an item, optionally with a link to evidence and a note,
records it as evidence for your access controls. When every item is
done the checklist is completed. An open checklist past its due date is
overdue. Overdue offboarding sorts to the top of the list, and everyone
in the organisation is notified once about each overdue offboarding.

## Who can do this

Admins and GRC engineers see checklists, start them and complete
items. Other roles don't see this page.
